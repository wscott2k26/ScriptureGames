Privacy Policy

This policy describes the local-first Scripture Games app published by Storm and Me LLC.

Information stored on the device

The app may store player names, selected emblems, reading path, game progress, scores, streaks, achievements, Genesis choices, family profiles, optional parent email, preferences, and Bible Companion conversation history. These records are used to provide the app’s features and are stored locally on the device in the current release.

Information collected by us

The current release does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, public social features, active payment processing, or an enabled cloud account system. We therefore do not receive the locally stored gameplay records through the app.

When you voluntarily email support, we receive the information included in that message and use it to respond and troubleshoot.

Children and Family Hub

Family Hub lets a parent or guardian create local child profiles. The current app does not knowingly transmit those profiles to us. Parents and guardians should supervise use and avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Bible Companion

The current companion uses curated on-device responses and stores conversation history locally. It should not be used to submit private, sensitive, emergency, medical, legal, or mental-health information.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. The current release does not share locally stored gameplay data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

Local records remain until they are deleted through the app, removed by clearing app storage, or removed by uninstalling the app. The Settings screen includes options to reset Genesis progress or erase all Scripture Games data on that device.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a local-first mobile application. No storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Device passcodes, operating-system updates, and secure account practices help protect local information.

Future changes

If a future release enables cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or purchases, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
