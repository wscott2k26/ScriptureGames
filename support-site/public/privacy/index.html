Privacy Policy

Scripture Games is published by Storm and Me LLC. It provides Bible-learning games, Scripture study, guided Faith Journeys, private prayer tools, family profiles, and a typed-and-voice Bible companion while collecting as little information as reasonably possible.

Guest and local-first use

You can choose Continue on This Device and use the app without creating an account. The complete public-domain World English Bible and the core app remain available locally after installation.

The app may store player names or nicknames, avatars, reading mode, game and journey progress, XP, streaks, achievements, Bible bookmarks, highlights, notes, sermon notes, reading history, private Faith Journey journals, private Prayer Garden entries, optional Family Hub information, Lumi conversation text, and app preferences in the device’s private application storage.

Optional cloud account and backup

Cloud backup is optional. If you create a Scripture Games cloud account, Supabase Auth processes your email address and password and assigns an account ID. When you deliberately choose Back Up This Device, a Scripture Games snapshot is sent to the production Supabase backend. The snapshot may include the local information listed above, including prayers, journals, notes, companion text, family records, and progress. The bundled Bible text is not uploaded.

Cloud credentials and authentication session data are excluded from the backup payload. Database row-level security restricts an authenticated account to its own backup. Backup and restore are explicit actions; this release does not silently merge competing device copies.

Lumi voice input and spoken replies

Lumi offers optional press-to-talk input and spoken replies. Scripture Games requests microphone and speech-recognition permission only when you choose press-to-talk. Your device’s speech-recognition service converts speech into text. Depending on the device and its settings, recognition may occur on the device or through the operating-system provider’s speech service.

Scripture Games does not intentionally save microphone audio recordings. Recognized text becomes the question shown in Lumi and may be stored in local conversation history and included in an optional cloud backup. Spoken replies use voices supplied by the device operating system. Typed chat remains available when permission is declined or speech recognition is unavailable.

Children and families

A parent or guardian should supervise children’s use of cloud backup, Lumi, Prayer Garden, and Faith Journey journals. Use nicknames rather than full legal names and do not enter sensitive personal, medical, safety, school, or contact information. Lumi is not an emergency service, therapist, medical provider, or substitute for a trusted adult, pastor, counselor, or qualified professional.

Advertising, analytics, and sale of data

This release contains no advertising SDK, advertising tracking, data-broker SDK, or third-party marketing analytics. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Local records remain until you erase them through Settings, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app, subject to platform backup and restore behavior.

To delete a cloud account and remote backup, open Settings → Cloud Backup → Delete Cloud Account. Local device data remains until you separately choose Erase All Scripture Games Data.

Security

Local data is stored in the device’s private application storage. Optional cloud backups use authenticated ownership and row-level database security. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so enter only information appropriate for an app backup.

Support messages

If you voluntarily email support, we receive the information included in that message and use it to respond and troubleshoot.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised for material changes.
