How can we help?

For technical support, email loftlatte25@gmail.com.

Include in your message

  • iPhone or Android device model
  • Operating-system version
  • Scripture Games app version and build number
  • The screen or trial where the issue occurred
  • Steps that reproduce the problem
  • A screenshot when it does not expose private information

Progress and offline play

The current release is local-first. Progress normally remains on the device and the embedded gameplay works without an internet connection after installation. Deleting the app, erasing app data, or using the in-app full reset can permanently remove local records.

Bible Companion

The companion offers curated, general Scripture guidance. It is not emergency, medical, legal, mental-health, or pastoral care. For urgent safety concerns, contact a trusted nearby person or appropriate local emergency support.

Response time

Support requests are reviewed as soon as reasonably possible. Store-review and beta feedback may also be submitted through TestFlight or Google Play testing channels.
