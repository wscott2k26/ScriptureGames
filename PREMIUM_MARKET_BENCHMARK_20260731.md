# Scripture Games Premium Market Benchmark — July 31, 2026

This benchmark compares Scripture Games with leading Bible, prayer, audio, study, and Scripture-game products. It is a product-direction record, not permission to copy another app's branding, copyrighted content, or proprietary experience.

## Market leaders and the user habits they reinforce

### YouVersion Bible App

Official product page: https://www.bible.com/app

- Audio Bible
- Daily verse, devotional, and guided prayer
- Multi-day Plans
- Discovery by topic
- Highlights and private notes
- Public product page reports 20M ratings and a 4.9 rating

**Lesson:** A complete Bible becomes habitual when it is paired with guided daily pathways and personal study tools.

### Hallow

Official feature pages:
- https://hallow.com/features/
- https://help.hallow.com/en/articles/9650908-overview-of-the-mobile-app

- Large audio-guided prayer library
- Bible reading and daily readings
- Custom prayer routines, reminders, goals, and streaks
- Journaling
- Prayer intentions, groups, challenges, and chat
- Voice guides, session lengths, music, and offline downloads

**Lesson:** High-quality audio, private reflection, and intentional routines create a premium feeling. Public community features add major moderation and privacy obligations.

### Dwell

Official product page: https://dwellapp.io

- Audio-first Scripture experience
- More than 20 genuine voices
- Listening plans, topical playlists, stories, and passages
- Independent voice/music controls
- Original music and artwork

**Lesson:** Voice choice and listening convenience are premium differentiators, but professional recorded narration is expensive and storage-heavy.

### BibleProject

Official app page: https://bibleproject.com/app/

- Guided journeys
- Videos, podcasts, articles, and classes
- Progress tracking across learning content
- Bible reader with related resources and reading history

**Lesson:** A visible learning path and "continue where you left off" structure help users move from isolated content into deeper understanding.

### Glorify

Store listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glorify-devotional-prayer/id1490587079

- Daily devotional and prayer rhythm
- In-app journal
- Topic-based spiritual support
- Community annotations and shared insights

**Lesson:** Users value a complete daily worship rhythm and a private place to process what they read.

### Strong Scripture game competitors

Representative store listings:
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bible-trivia-christian-game/id1532616861
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/biblechat-trivia/id6760339598
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bible-trivia-app-game/id626342259

Common competitive features:

- Thousands of questions across books and topics
- Multiple difficulty levels
- Daily challenge and streaks
- Fill-in-the-blank and word puzzles
- XP, ranks, achievements, and progress breakdowns
- Multiplayer or global leaderboards
- Multiple translations and languages
- Cross-device sync or anonymous guest play

**Lesson:** Scripture Games already competes strongly on cinematic progression, complete offline Bible access, daily rhythm, family profiles, explanations, achievements, leagues, and cloud backup. The largest missing premium value was not more trivia—it was a deeper faith-life layer and a dependable voice companion.

## Premium release implemented from this benchmark

1. **Faith Journeys**
   - Four seven-day journeys; 28 days total
   - Scripture, reflection, prayer, action, and private journal prompt
   - Offline progress and optional cloud backup

2. **Prayer Garden**
   - Private requests by category
   - Active and answered-prayer history
   - Delete and return-to-praying controls
   - Offline-first and optional cloud backup

3. **Lumi voice conversation**
   - Composer lifted above the floating tab bar
   - Typed chat always remains available
   - Native press-to-talk with explicit permission
   - Enhanced device-voice replies
   - Per-message Listen/Stop control
   - No microphone recording persisted by Scripture Games

4. **Listen to Chapter**
   - Offline Bible chapter narration through the device voice
   - Chunked narration for long chapters
   - No giant audio download or paid narration dependency

## Honest release boundary

This release makes Lumi a usable typed-and-voice Scripture companion, but its current knowledge engine remains curated and local-first. It must not be marketed as an all-knowing pastor, therapist, emergency service, or unrestricted generative AI. A future generative backend requires source-grounded answers, theological review, crisis handling, cost limits, privacy disclosure, and a server-side secret boundary before release.
