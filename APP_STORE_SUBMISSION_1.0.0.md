### Description

Scripture Games is a biblical competition-adventure built for daily study, family learning, and practical use in church.

ENTER THE GENESIS TOURNAMENT
Journey through ten cinematic trials covering Genesis 1–50. Read the briefing, make story decisions, answer Scripture questions, unlock the next gate, and rise toward the Genesis Champion Hall.

BUILD A DAILY FAITH RHYTHM
Complete the Daily Bread Run across five rotating Scripture fields. Grow your Faith Flame, use a Grace Leaf when life interrupts your routine, earn XP, and progress through Seed, Lamp, Lion, and Crown weekly leagues.

READ THE COMPLETE BIBLE OFFLINE
Access all 66 books of the public-domain World English Bible without requiring an internet connection after installation. Jump directly to a reference, search Scripture, move between books and chapters, and continue where you stopped.

USE IT IN CHURCH
Switch on large-text Church Mode, bookmark verses, add private notes, highlight passages in four colors, save chapter-linked sermon notes, and share verses through the device’s native share sheet.

LEARN THROUGH PLAY
Train across 15 Bible topics, explore illustrated Bible stories, practice memory passages, solve word puzzles, complete devotionals, unlock achievements, and review clear Scripture references after answers.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
Create local family profiles, switch between players, track seven-day learning activity, and compete on a leaderboard containing only real profiles saved on the device.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Scripture Games is local-first. It contains no ads, analytics SDK, public chat, active purchase flow, or required account registration. Player progress, notes, bookmarks, highlights, family profiles, and settings stay on the device.

Features include:
- Complete 66-book offline World English Bible
- Bible reference lookup and full-text search
- Bookmarks, highlights, reading history, and private verse notes
- Church Mode and chapter-linked sermon notes
- Ten-trial Genesis Tournament
- Daily Bread challenge and forgiving Faith Flame rhythm
- Weekly Seed, Lamp, Lion, and Crown leagues
- 168 training questions across 15 topics
- Illustrated stories, memory passages, puzzles, and devotionals
- Local family profiles and achievements
- Reduced-motion, haptic, and cinematic-text controls

Scripture Games uses the public-domain World English Bible. It is designed as a study and learning companion and is not affiliated with a specific church or denomination.

## App Review notes

Scripture Games is a local-first Bible-learning app and game. No login is required.

Recommended review path:
1. Launch the app and create a local player.
2. Open **Bible** from the main tab bar.
3. Search for `John 3:16` or any Scripture phrase.
4. Open a verse to test bookmark, highlight, private note, and share actions.
5. Enable **Church Mode** to verify the larger reading presentation.
6. Save a chapter-linked sermon note.
7. Open **Command** and start the Daily Bread Run.
8. Open **Tournament** to view the Genesis progression map.
9. Open **Training** to test Bible-topic quizzes.

The complete World English Bible is bundled for offline use. The application contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, external AI service, public user-generated content, or active purchase flow. All player profiles, progress, family information, bookmarks, highlights, and notes are stored locally on the device. The app’s optional family email field, if entered, remains local and is not transmitted.

The support and privacy policy are available at `https://scripture-games-support.vercel.app/`.
