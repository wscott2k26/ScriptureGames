# Scripture Games — Bible & Church Companion Release

Release source merged: July 31, 2026

## Included

- Daily Bread five-field Scripture challenge
- Faith Flame streak rhythm and Grace Leaves
- Seed, Lamp, Lion, and Crown seven-day leagues
- Continue Where You Left Off for Genesis Season
- Shareable Witness Cards and stronger answer feedback
- Complete 66-book offline World English Bible
- Quick reference lookup and full-text Scripture search
- Bookmarks, four-color highlights, private verse notes, and reading history
- Large-text Church Mode
- Chapter-linked private sermon notes
- Bible as a primary app tab with the illustrated Story Archive preserved

## Release safeguards

Every release build must generate the Bible from the official public-domain eBible.org WEBP archive and pass:

- 66 books
- 1,189 chapters
- at least 31,000 verses
- content audit
- visual audit
- Expo Doctor
- TypeScript
- ESLint
- iOS export
- Android export

The app must never represent the empty development fallback as full Bible access.
