## Anti-redundancy rule

Before starting a new product or rebuilding an existing feature, inspect the current repository, deployment, prior audit, and latest release status. Finish the active milestone before opening another major workstream unless a production outage or legal deadline requires otherwise.

## Scripture Games active release scope

- Daily Bread Run with five rotating Scripture fields.
- Faith Flame daily rhythm with forgiving Grace Leaves.
- Seed, Lamp, Lion, and Crown weekly leagues.
- Continue Where You Left Off for the Genesis tournament.
- Stronger answer feedback, Scripture context, elapsed time, and shareable Witness Cards.
- Full offline Bible access with quick reference lookup, keyword search, bookmarks, highlights, private verse notes, reading history, large-text Church Mode, and chapter-linked sermon notes.
- The World English Bible source text must remain unaltered and every release build must verify 66 books, 1,189 chapters, and at least 31,000 verses.

## Scripture Games product direction

Scripture Games should combine biblical accuracy, calm daily practice, satisfying game feel, visible progress, forgiving habit support, family-friendly competition, shareable encouragement, and practical use during personal study or church. It must borrow proven interaction principles without copying another app's branding, artwork, text, layouts, or proprietary content.
